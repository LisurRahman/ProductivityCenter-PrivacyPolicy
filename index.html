Privacy Policy for Productivity Center Extension

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Productivity Center is a browser extension for time management, task lists, website blocking, and local activity summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what the extension accesses and how that information is handled.

Key takeaway: The extension processes and stores its data locally in browser extension storage. It does not send your data to a developer-controlled server, use analytics, or sell your information.

1. What Information Does the Extension Use?

To provide its features, the extension uses the following information locally in your browser:

  • Website domains and time: When an accessible active tab has a web address, the extension records its hostname and increments a daily activity counter.
  • Tasks: Task text and completion state entered in the Quick tasks feature.
  • Settings: Timer state and durations, focus-mode state, and your blocked-site list.

We do NOT collect: Your passwords, credit card numbers, personal messages, search history, or any content from the websites you visit.

2. Where Is Your Information Stored?

The information above is stored exclusively in the browser's local extension storage. The extension does not use a remote database or cloud sync service.

  • Your data is stored only on your own computer.
  • We do not have access to your data.
  • It is not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

3. How Do We Use Your Data?

We use this data solely to power the extension's features:

  • Daily Productivity Stats: To show you a report of how much time you spent on specific sites.
  • Website Blocker and Activity Tracking: The extension requests access to all website addresses during installation. It uses this access to record the active tab's hostname for local activity summaries and to block only the sites you add while Focus Mode is enabled. It does not read page contents.
  • Pomodoro Timer: To track your work and break intervals.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. No third-party advertisers, analytics companies, or marketing firms have access to your data.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can view your stats and settings at any time within the extension.
  • You can delete all locally stored extension data by clicking "Clear all data" in the popup. This also resets the timer and removes active custom blocking rules.
  • Removing the extension normally removes its locally stored data according to the browser's extension-storage behavior.

6. Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, it is protected from external servers. However, standard browser security applies. We recommend keeping your browser and operating system up to date.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the extension publisher through the store listing where you installed Productivity Center.
